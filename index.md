# Magnet Wars Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 15, 2026

Magnet Wars ("the app", "we", or "us") is a polarity strategy game. This
policy describes what the app stores and shares when optional online features
are used.

## Information stored on your device

Local games, campaign progress, achievements, settings, audio preferences,
replays, and accessibility preferences are stored on the device. Local play
does not require an internet connection. Removing the app removes this local
data unless Android backup restores it.

## Optional online features

You can play locally without an account. If you use online profiles,
leaderboards, invitations, or online matches, the app can use Firebase
Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging, and
Firebase Analytics. Depending on the feature, these services process:

- Authentication identifiers and account details supplied by you, such as an
  email address, display name, or Google account identity. Guest accounts use a
  Firebase-generated identifier.
- Profile, score, achievement, friend/invitation, match, move, and presence
  data needed to provide the selected online feature.
- A Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token and notification preferences
  when match or invitation notifications are enabled.
- Usage and diagnostic events such as sign-in method, game mode, match
  outcome, score, duration, and achievement type. Analytics may also collect
  standard app-instance, device, and diagnostic information as described by
  Google Firebase.

Online data is sent only when the related feature is used. Firebase may
process data on servers operated by Google under Google's terms and privacy
policy. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

## Nearby multiplayer

The optional Nearby multiplayer mode uses Android Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and
nearby-device APIs to discover and connect to another nearby device. Android
may require location-related permissions for discovery; Magnet Wars does not
use those permissions to determine or store your physical location. Nearby
game data is exchanged between participating devices for the match.

## Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the app can display match and
invitation updates. You can disable notifications in Android settings or in
the app where available.

## Sharing and retention

Online data is shared with the Firebase services required for the feature you
choose and with the other players who need it to participate in a match or
social feature. We retain online account and game data while the account or
feature remains active, or as needed to operate and protect the service.
Local data remains on your device until you remove it or clear the app's
storage.

## Your choices and deletion requests

You may play without signing in, disable notifications, and decline optional
nearby permissions. To request deletion of an online profile and associated
data, contact us from the address below with enough information to identify
the account. We will verify the request before processing it.
